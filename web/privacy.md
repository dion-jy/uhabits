# Looply Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-06-18**

Looply is a habit tracker for Android. It is a fork of the open-source
[Loop Habit Tracker](https://github.com/iSoron/uhabits) (uhabits) by Álinson
Santos Xavier, licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (or later).
Looply adds an optional cloud sync feature so that you, and an AI agent you
explicitly authorize, can read and write your habit data.

This policy explains exactly what data Looply collects, why, where it is
stored, and how you can delete it. We do not sell your data, we do not show
ads, and we do not embed third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

## 1. Local-only by default

Looply works fully offline. Your habits and check-ins are stored in a local
database on your device. If you never sign in and never enable cloud sync,
**no habit data ever leaves your device** and none of the cloud collection
described below applies to you. Local-only data stays on your device and is
removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Cloud sync is **opt-in**. It begins only after you take an explicit action in
the app (signing in with Google and/or enabling sync).

## 2. What we collect when cloud sync is enabled

When you enable cloud sync, Looply transmits the following to our backend
(see Section 4, "Where your data is stored"):

### a. Account / identity data
- **Google account email address** and a **user ID** (a random UUID issued by
  our authentication provider) — obtained when you sign in with Google. We use
  these solely to identify your account, tie your data to you, and let your
  data survive a reinstall or move to a new device.
- A **device ID** — a random identifier (a UUID) generated by the app on first
  use. It is **not** a hardware serial, advertising ID, or any
  Google-resettable device identifier; it only distinguishes one installation
  of the app from another so sync and restore work correctly.

### b. Habit data
- **Habit definitions:** the habit's name, description/notes, question text,
  color, position/ordering, schedule/frequency, type, target value, target
  type, unit, archived state, and a stable habit UUID.
- **Check-in entries:** for each time you mark a habit, the timestamp, the
  recorded value, any note you attached, and a source tag indicating whether
  the entry came from the app or from your authorized agent.

We collect this so your habit history is backed up, synced across your
devices, and accessible to an AI agent you authorize (Section 3).

### c. Coaching messages
- **Coaching / nudge messages** generated by an AI agent you have authorized,
  which the app fetches to display to you, along with their read state and
  optional metadata.

### d. App heartbeat (diagnostic / sync-status data)
- A small **heartbeat** record reporting your device ID, your habit count,
  your entry count, the timestamp of your most recent entry, and the **app
  version name**. This lets the app and your authorized agent detect when local
  and cloud data are out of sync. It is not used for advertising or sold to
  anyone.

### What we do NOT collect
- No advertising identifiers, no location, no contacts, no third-party
  analytics, no biometric data, no payment information through the app.

## 3. AI agent access (the agent-token model)

Looply's purpose is to let an AI agent you control read and write your habit
data on your behalf (for example, to check off a habit or send you a coaching
nudge).

- From within the app you can generate an **agent secret token**. The token is
  bound to your account.
- A configured agent presents this token (as an `x-agent-secret` request
  header) to the backend. Database row-level security restricts that token to
  **only your own** habits, entries, coaching messages, and heartbeat — it
  cannot access any other user's data.
- The agent acts on your behalf. You choose whether to enable it and which
  agent you share the token with. You can revoke an agent's access at any time
  from the app (which clears the secret on the server), after which the token
  no longer works.

You are responsible for keeping your agent token confidential, the same way
you would protect a password.

## 4. Where your data is stored (data processor)

Cloud data is stored in **Supabase** (a hosted PostgreSQL platform) acting as
our data processor / sub-processor. Data is held in the Supabase project's
configured hosting region. Supabase processes the data on our behalf to
provide storage, authentication (Google sign-in), and API access. We do not
authorize Supabase to use your data for its own purposes.

Authentication (Google sign-in) is handled through Supabase Auth's Google
OAuth integration. Google processes your sign-in under
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. How your data is used

We use the data described above only to:
- provide cloud backup and cross-device sync of your habits and check-ins;
- authenticate you and keep your data tied to your account across reinstalls;
- enable an AI agent you authorize to read and write your habit data;
- deliver coaching/nudge messages to you;
- detect and resolve sync inconsistencies (via the heartbeat).

We do **not** sell or rent your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for
advertising or third-party analytics.

## 6. Data sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties except:
- **Supabase**, as the hosting/storage and authentication processor described
  in Section 4;
- **Google**, for the sign-in flow you initiate;
- the **AI agent you yourself authorize** via the agent token; and
- where required by law.

## 7. Data retention

- Your habit data, entries, coaching messages, device link records, and
  heartbeat are retained in the cloud for as long as your account exists and
  cloud sync remains enabled, so the data is available across your devices.
- When you request deletion (Section 8), the associated records are removed.
- Local data on your device persists until you delete it in the app, clear the
  app's data, or uninstall the app.

## 8. Deleting your data

You can delete your cloud data and account in two ways:

**a. From the app**
Sign out and/or use the in-app option to delete your habits/data. Uninstalling
the app removes all local-only data from your device, but uninstalling alone
does **not** remove data already stored in the cloud.

**b. Web request (no reinstall required)**
Visit our account deletion page and send the deletion request as described:
**[delete-account.html](./delete-account.html)**

On a verified deletion request we delete the records associated with your
account from the cloud, including:
- your habit definitions (`habits`),
- your check-in entries (`entries`),
- your coaching/nudge messages (`coaching`),
- your device link / agent-token records (`device_links`),
- your app heartbeat record (`device_heartbeat`), and
- your authentication user record.

We process deletion requests within **30 days**. Some information may persist
briefly in routine encrypted backups before being overwritten on the normal
backup cycle.

## 9. Security

- Data in transit is protected with HTTPS/TLS.
- Access to your cloud data is enforced by database row-level security keyed to
  your account, and (for agents) to your account-bound agent token.
- The device ID is a random value and is not used to track you across other
  apps or services.

## 10. Children

Looply is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect data from
children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will
delete it.
