Delete your Looply account & data

Looply · AI-connected habit tracker

You can request deletion of your Looply account and all of your cloud-stored data here, without reinstalling the app.

What gets deleted

On a verified request, we remove the records tied to your account from our cloud database, including:

  • Habits (habits) — your habit definitions: name, notes, question, color, schedule, target, etc.
  • Check-in entries (entries) — your logged completions: timestamps, values, and notes.
  • Coaching messages (coaching) — nudges and messages from your authorized AI agent.
  • Device links & agent tokens (device_links) — the records that pair your devices and AI agent to your account.
  • App heartbeat (device_heartbeat) — the sync-status record for your device(s).
  • Your account — your authentication user record (Google email + user ID).

How to request deletion

Send your request using the button below. Please send it from the same Google account email you signed in to Looply with, so we can verify ownership.

Prefer to do it in the app?

You can also sign out and delete your data from within Looply. Note that uninstalling the app removes only the data stored locally on your device — it does not delete data already stored in the cloud, so use this page (or the in-app delete option) to remove cloud data.
